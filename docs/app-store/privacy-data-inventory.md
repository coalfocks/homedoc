It must be checked against the exact release binary, backend configuration, and provider settings before publishing labels. The RevenueCat 10.15 release integration and proposed paid tier described below are not verified in a live build.

## Provisional answer summary

- **Data collection:** Yes. Account, home-record, photo, and user-requested AI data are processed by the app and service providers.
- **Tracking:** No tracking behavior is evident in reviewed source. Recheck all included SDKs and the final binary before selecting “not used for tracking.”
- **Data linked to the user:** Yes for account records and content stored under the authenticated account.
- **Purposes:** App Functionality and Account Management; Purchases may apply if the proposed subscription is enabled. Customer Support depends on whether feedback email is received and retained by HomeDoc.

## Questions to resolve before publishing App Privacy

1. Does the exact release build or a transitive SDK collect diagnostics, crash logs, device identifiers, or analytics not identified here?
2. What data does the integrated RevenueCat 10.15 SDK actually collect or share in the configured build, and what identifiers are used? Verify against the SDK's current disclosures and production settings.
3. Are property addresses consistently optional, and should they be declared as Physical Address as well as user-generated content?
4. What OpenAI retention settings apply to production prompts and responses?
5. Will paid access be enabled in the submitted build, and do App Store products, server verification, and the purchase UI match the privacy disclosures?
6. Does the support mailbox retain feedback and prefilled account details, and who can access them?
7. Are sharing, transfer, and contractor access enabled for the release audience?
