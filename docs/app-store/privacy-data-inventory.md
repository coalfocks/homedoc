## Provisional answers

- Data collection: Yes. Account, home records, photos, user-requested AI, usage, and purchase status are processed by HomeDoc and its service providers.
- Linked to user: Yes for the account/content/purchase categories above. Purchase `appAccountToken` intentionally maps to the HomeDoc account.
- Tracking: No tracking, ads, IDFA, or advertising-attribution SDK is evident in the release source. Confirm transitive dependencies and the exact build before publishing this answer.
- Privacy manifest: first-party collected-data entries declare Name, Email Address, Physical Address, Photos or Videos, Customer Support, Other User Content, User ID, Purchase History, and Product Interaction as linked to the user, not used for tracking, and collected for App Functionality. No tracking domains are declared. App Store Connect privacy answers are a separate disclosure and must match actual practices.
- Purposes: App Functionality. No subscription analytics service is included; do not copy labels from the superseded third-party SDK design.
